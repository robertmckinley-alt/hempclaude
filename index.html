Before you run traffic

  • All three share one config. Deadline date and every product link live in the window.SITE block at the top of assets/site.js. Change them once, all pages update.
  • Swap in your tracked affiliate URLs. Every CTA reads its href from data-link="BUY", so replacing SITE.BUY updates all of them.
  • The testimonials on page 02 are empty slots on purpose. They are marked with dashed borders. Paste real, verbatim reviews from your own verified platform — fabricated testimonials are the single fastest way to draw an FTC action.
  • Verify pricing before launch. Prices were taken from the product page at build time and change often.
  • If the December 11 delay is ever enacted, set DEADLINE to '2026-12-11T00:00:00-05:00' and flip DELAY_ENACTED to true.
